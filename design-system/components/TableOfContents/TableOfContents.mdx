## Rules

- Items match the block headings on the page, in order. Each item links to its block's `id`.
- The title "On this page" uses `subsection-title`; items use `link`.
- The list has a 2px `border-divider` line on the left. The active item gets a 2px `border-emphasis` line on top of it and `surface-accent` text. The 2px width comes from Figma, which is wider than the 1px border token.
- Without `activeId`, the active item follows the scroll position. Choosing an item in the bar closes the overlay.
