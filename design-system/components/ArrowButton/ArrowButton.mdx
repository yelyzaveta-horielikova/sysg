# Arrow button

A round "go" cue (Figma "Arrows/Var.2", external library). Used in the corner of Sharing insights cards.

## Rules

- `white` (white circle, sg-medium arrow) on `sg-medium` or teal surfaces; `blue` (sg-medium circle, white arrow) on `surface-muted`.
- Inside a clickable card it is decorative: omit `onClick` and `label`. On its own, pass both.
- Radius `full`.
