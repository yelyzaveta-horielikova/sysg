# Expert quote

A statement from a SysGears expert or leader (Figma "expert-quote-card"). No card fill.

<Canvas of={S.Default} />
<Controls of={S.Default} />

## Layout

| Width | Layout |
|---|---|
| ≥1024 viewport | 244×264 avatar beside the quote, 64px apart, 64px side padding |
| below 1024 | quote, then a 74×80 avatar with name and role; 16px side padding |

## Rules

- One per block (Expert-quote pattern).
- The photo is a cut-out portrait with a transparent background. It sits in an `sg-medium` circle, and the head rises above the circle.
- Quote, name and role use `body-article`; the role is in `text-secondary` (black at 70%, as on the website).
