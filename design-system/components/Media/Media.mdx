# Media

An image, a video, or a placeholder for one. Radius `md`, full width of its column.

<Canvas of={S.ImagePlaceholder} />
<Controls of={S.ImagePlaceholder} />

## Rules

- `kind="video"` always plays by itself, muted, looped and inline. There are no controls.
- `alt` is required: in case studies the media carries meaning.
- Without `ratio` a file keeps its natural height. `ratio` crops it to one of the sanctioned ratios (`layout.md` → Alignment rules).
- Without `src` it shows a placeholder (`surface-muted`, divider border, "Image" or "Video" in `caption`), 2:1 unless a `ratio` is set.
- Inside a card use `Card` with `media={{ kind: 'image', … }}`.
