# Badge

A tag for a technology, industry or category: text only, a 1px border and no fill. This is the local Figma "Badge" (UI Kit › Cards).

<Canvas of={BadgeStories.Group} />
<Controls of={BadgeStories.Medium} />

## Rules

- `medium` (padding 8/12, radius md) is the default; use `small` (4/8, radius sm) for dense contexts.
- `black` on light surfaces, `white` on images or dark surfaces.
- Group badges with `BadgeGroup` (wrap, 10px gap).
- Never recreate the external-library "Badge" (Size=Regular).
