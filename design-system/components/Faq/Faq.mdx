# FAQ

The FAQ block: a Section heading followed by questions (Figma FAQ block + "faq-text"). Max 1240px wide, single column at every size. `FaqItem` is a single question (`subsection-title`, h3) with its answer (`body-article`) 16px below.

<Canvas of={S.Default} />
<Controls of={S.Default} />

## Rules

- Items and the heading are 60px apart (`spacing.section.M`).
- Answers are always visible: Figma shows no accordion.
- Usually the last block before the form or footer (inferred).
