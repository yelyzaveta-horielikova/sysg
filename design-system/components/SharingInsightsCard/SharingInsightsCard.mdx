# Sharing insights card

A teaser for a blog article with its author (Figma "Sharing-insights-card"). The whole card is a link.

<Canvas of={S.Row} />
<Controls of={S.Accent} />

## Structure

- Article title (`subsection-title`) at the top.
- Square author photo (1:1, half the card width, max 240px) flush with the bottom-left corner, with name and role (`body-article`) beside it.
- Arrow button in the bottom-right corner (24px from the edges).
- Radius md; at least 340px tall.

## Tones

| Tone | Background | Text | Arrow button |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accent` | sg-medium | white | white |
| `muted` | surface-muted | dark | blue |
| `teal` | teal | dark | white |

## Rules

- Three cards per row from a 1024 viewport, one column below (Card grid pattern, `gap.XS`).
- Mix tones within a row as the content needs; the tone follows the article or author.
