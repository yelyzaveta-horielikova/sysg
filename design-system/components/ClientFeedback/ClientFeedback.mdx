# Client feedback

A single client quote from a review platform (Figma "Client-feedback").

<Canvas of={S.Default} />
<Controls of={S.Default} />

## Structure

- `surface-muted` fill with a 1px `border-emphasis` line on the **left only**. No corner radius. Padding 30/20 (`inset.sm` / `inset.xs`), 40px between parts.
- **Header:** the platform logo (26px) and name (22/1.3 Light) on the left; the rating (18/1.4 ExtraLight) and a star on the right. Both sizes are off-scale website values, hard-coded in the component.
- **Quote** in `body-article`.
- **Author:** an optional 72px photo (radius `sm`), the name in `body-article` and the role in `caption`.

## Rules

- One quote per card.
- Used in the Client-feedback block (next to a `side` Section heading, stacked below 1024) and in Feedback & video (`patterns.md`).
- The card fills the width it is given; the block sets the width.
- Clutch shows its colour logo, as in Figma. Upwork and GoodFirms use the monochrome logos in the text colour, because Figma has no colour versions of them.
